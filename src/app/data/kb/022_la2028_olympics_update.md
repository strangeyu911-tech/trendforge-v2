Organizers of the Los Angeles 2028 Olympics said Thursday that 85% of competition venues have completed readiness inspections, and that 3.2 million tickets were sold in the first public sales phase, the strongest early demand since London 2012.

The organizing committee confirmed its operating budget remains at $7.1 billion, fully privately funded, with sponsorship revenue reaching $2.9 billion against a $2.5 billion target. Two new venues — a temporary beach-stadium in Santa Monica and the upgraded SoFi aquatic facility — will host test events this October.

"With two years to go, we are ahead of schedule on venues and ahead of budget on revenue," said Dana Whitfield, chief executive of LA28. She said the Games will use 28 existing or temporary venues, with no permanent stadium construction, keeping to the committee's "radical reuse" pledge.

Flag football and cricket, making their Olympic debuts in 2028, sold out their initial ticket allocations within six hours, officials said. Total ticket inventory across all sessions is approximately 14 million.

The International Olympic Committee's coordination commission gave the preparations its highest readiness rating to date. An estimated 15,000 athletes from 206 delegations are expected to compete across 36 sports.
