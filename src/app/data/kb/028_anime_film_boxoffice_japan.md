Studio Kagerou's animated feature "Voyage of Stardust" has earned 18.2 billion yen ($123 million) at the Japanese box office in nine weeks, distributor Toho announced Sunday, making it the highest-grossing domestic film of 2026 to date.

The film, directed by Haruki Mori, has sold 12.1 million admissions since its May 30 opening and held the top weekend spot for six consecutive weeks. Overseas, it has grossed $86 million across 54 markets, with a North American release through GKIDS set for August 21.

"Audiences connected with a story about a lighthouse keeper and a fallen satellite — hand-drawn warmth in a year of synthetic spectacle," Mori said at a stage greeting in Tokyo. The production took five years and 180,000 hand-drawn frames, the studio said.

The success caps a strong year for Japanese animation: industry tracker Box Office Mojo data show anime titles captured 44% of Japan's first-half box office, and the Association of Japanese Animations projects the global anime market will reach $41 billion in 2026, up 12% year over year.

"Voyage of Stardust" has been selected as Japan's entry for the Academy Awards' international feature category. Streaming rights were acquired for a reported $28 million, with a global digital release planned for January 2027.
