Google on Wednesday released Gemini 3 Pro, its new flagship model, touting a two-million-token context window and state-of-the-art scores on a range of reasoning benchmarks.

The model scored 94.2% on the MMLU-Pro benchmark and 89.1% on the SWE-bench Verified coding evaluation, according to Google's technical report, edging past OpenAI's o4 and Anthropic's Claude 5 on both tests. Google said inference costs fell 55% compared with Gemini 2.5 Pro.

"We designed Gemini 3 for long-horizon agentic work, not just chat," said Kavitha Rao, vice president of Google DeepMind, in a briefing. She said the model can sustain coherent multi-step tasks for "more than eight hours" of simulated work time.

The release is tightly integrated with Google Workspace: starting in August, enterprise customers can run Gemini 3 agents inside Docs, Sheets, and Gmail under existing data-residency agreements. Google said Workspace AI subscriptions have passed 11 million paid seats, up 76% year over year.

Independent testing firm Artificial Analysis ranked Gemini 3 Pro first in its intelligence index but noted it trails Claude 5 on instruction-following for creative writing. API pricing is set at $1.25 per million input tokens, 30% below its predecessor.
