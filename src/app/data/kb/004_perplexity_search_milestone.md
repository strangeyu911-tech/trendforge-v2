Perplexity AI said Monday its answer engine processed 220 million queries in June, triple the volume of a year ago, as AI-native search continues to chip away at incumbents.

The San Francisco startup, last valued at $18 billion in a March funding round, also disclosed that its Comet browser has reached 30 million monthly active users since launching in February. Comet's built-in agent can book flights, fill forms, and summarize pages across open tabs.

"Search is becoming a task-completion engine, not a list of links," said Maya Chen, Perplexity's head of product, in an interview. She said average session length on Comet is 19 minutes, compared with under two minutes for a conventional search visit.

Data from analytics firm Statcounter shows Google's share of global search referrals slipped to 86.4% in June, down from 91.2% eighteen months earlier, with AI answer engines capturing most of the difference. Google has responded by expanding its own AI Overviews to 190 countries.

Perplexity's advertising business, launched late last year, is on pace for $340 million in annual revenue, according to a person familiar with the figures. The company expects to reach 500 million monthly queries by December, Chen said.
