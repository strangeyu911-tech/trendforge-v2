Nova Raye's Aurora World Tour has grossed $1.4 billion across 112 stadium shows, promoter Live Nation confirmed Friday, surpassing the previous all-time touring record with 38 dates still remaining.

The 27-year-old singer sold 9.8 million tickets over 14 months, playing to stadium crowds on five continents. Average ticket price was $143, with VIP packages selling out in a median of nine minutes, according to Live Nation data. The tour's three-night stand at Wembley Stadium drew 270,000 attendees, a venue record.

"Routing a show of this scale — 84 trucks, a 60-meter kinetic stage — is a logistics feat as much as an artistic one," said Marcus Webb, Live Nation's president of global touring. He estimated the tour generated $2.6 billion in broader economic activity for host cities.

Raye's album "Aurora" returned to number one on the Billboard 200 for a 14th nonconsecutive week, and her concert film, shot at the São Paulo finale for 210,000 fans, will stream exclusively in December. Merchandise sales per attendee averaged $38, the highest per-capita figure the promoter has recorded.

The tour concludes in Tokyo on November 21. Pollstar projects a final gross near $1.6 billion.
