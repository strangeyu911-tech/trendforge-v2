Real Madrid have completed the signing of 19-year-old Brazilian striker Kauan Ferreira from Palmeiras for a fee of €120 million, the Spanish club announced Tuesday, making him the most expensive teenager in football history.

Ferreira, who scored 34 goals in 41 appearances this season and led Palmeiras to the Copa Libertadores semifinals, signed a six-year contract worth a reported €14 million per year. The deal includes a €1 billion release clause, matching the club's standard for marquee players.

"Kauan combines elite finishing with a pressing intensity that fits our transition game," said Real Madrid sporting director Javier Calleja at the unveiling, attended by 38,000 fans at the Santiago Bernabéu. The club said his number 9 shirt sold 400,000 units within 48 hours, a club record.

Palmeiras retain a 12% sell-on clause and will receive an additional €15 million in performance bonuses tied to Champions League goals. The São Paulo club called it "the largest transfer in South American football history," surpassing Neymar's €88 million move to Barcelona in 2013.

The signing takes Real Madrid's summer spending to €265 million. Manager Xabi Alonso said Ferreira will join pre-season immediately and could debut in the Spanish Super Cup on August 12.
