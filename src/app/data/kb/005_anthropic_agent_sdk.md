Anthropic on Tuesday released Claude Agent SDK 2.0, a toolkit for building production-grade autonomous agents, and said its enterprise customer count has crossed 10,000 organizations.

The new SDK adds long-term memory, deterministic tool-use replay, and a permissions framework that lets administrators cap what agents can spend or modify. Anthropic said early adopters cut manual steps in document-processing workflows by 63% on average.

One early customer, freight firm Nortrail, deployed Claude agents to reconcile shipping invoices across 14 countries. "What took a team of nine analysts three days now closes in four hours," said Tom Berger, Nortrail's chief technology officer. "The audit replay feature is what got our compliance team to sign off."

Anthropic's business has grown rapidly: people familiar with the matter say annualized revenue passed $7 billion in July, up from $2 billion at the start of 2025, driven largely by Claude Code and enterprise agent contracts. The company recently opened a 400-person office in Dublin to serve European clients.

Analysts say the agent platform contest is now a three-horse race among Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google. "The differentiator this year is reliability engineering, not benchmark scores," said Forrester analyst Priya Raman.
